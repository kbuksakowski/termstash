## How to sign

Add a line to `CONTRIBUTORS.md` in your first pull request, in this form:

```
- Full Name <email@example.com> (@github-handle) — signed the ICLA on YYYY-MM-DD
```

Adding that line means you have read this agreement and accept it for every contribution you
make to TermStash, now and in the future. You only do this once.

Contributing on behalf of a company, rather than as an individual? Ask in your pull request —
a Corporate CLA will be provided.
